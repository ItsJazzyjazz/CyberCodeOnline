In ancient CCO, two legendary warrior brothers, Jazzyjazz and HBUNKNOWN, fought over a single magical dumpling said to grant immense power. Crafted by Jazzyjazz for them to share, the dumpling sparked a fierce clash when HBUNKNOWN demanded it for himself. Their battle shook the heavens, and in the chaos, the dumpling was lost to the Abyss of Chaos, unleashing war across the world. Thus began an age of turmoil—all over one dumpling.
